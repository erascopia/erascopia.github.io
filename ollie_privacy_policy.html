Privacy Policy

Ollie Ant City is a cozy kids game. The app is designed to be simple and safe. It does not require accounts and does not provide chat or social features.

Last updated: January 2026 No accounts No third party ads No precise location

Quick summary

  • We do not require accounts (no sign-up).
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or contact lists.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • Game progress is stored locally on your device (and may be included in iCloud device backups if you enable iCloud backup in iOS).
  • In-app purchases (if offered) are handled by Apple (we do not receive payment details).

1) Information we store

Ollie Ant City may store the following information on your device:

  • Game progress (levels completed, unlocked story moments).
  • Game settings (music and sound preferences, accessibility options if available).
  • Local-only unlock state (for example, purchased/unlocked features saved on device).
Important: Ollie Ant City does not include social account login, chat, or user profiles.

2) Information we do not collect

Ollie Ant City does not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or contact lists
  • Photos, microphone recordings, or camera content
  • Precise location data
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA)
  • Social media logins, passwords, or tokens
  • Messages, chat content, or user-generated posts

3) How we use information

The game uses stored progress and settings to:

  • save and restore your game state
  • unlock story moments and mini games
  • remember your music and sound preferences
  • help us improve stability if diagnostics are enabled (see below)

4) Diagnostics

If the app uses Apple crash reports or basic diagnostics, they may include device type, iOS version, and crash logs. These reports do not include your name or contact info.

If you have not enabled sharing diagnostics with developers in iOS, fewer reports may be available.

5) Storage

By default, game data is stored locally on your device. If you enable iCloud backup on your device, iOS may include app data in your backups.

6) Permissions

  • Notifications: used only for optional reminders or gentle nudges (if the game provides them). If the game does not use notifications, you will not be asked for this permission.

You can manage permissions at any time in iOS Settings → Notifications → Ollie Ant City.

7) In-app purchases

If you purchase optional content, billing is processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment information. Apple may provide us purchase status (for example, whether content is unlocked) so the app can enable features.

  • Purchases can be managed in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions (for subscriptions) or your Apple purchase history (for one-time purchases).
  • If content does not unlock after reinstalling, use Restore Purchases in the app (if provided).

8) Terms of Use

Ollie Ant City uses Apple’s standard Terms of Use (EULA): https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/

9) Children’s privacy

Ollie Ant City is designed for kids and families and does not require accounts or collect personal identifiers. If you believe we have accidentally collected personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10) Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows when changes were made.
