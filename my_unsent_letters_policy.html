Privacy Policy

My Unsent Letters is built to stay private, quiet, and offline-first. The app stores letters on your device, uses local notifications only when you ask for reminders, and does not require an account.

Last updated: June 8, 2026 Local storage first No account required No AI or backend

Quick summary

  • Your letters stay on your device by default.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not require login to use the app.
  • Local reminders only activate if you choose to schedule them.

1) Information you provide

You may write a title, body, category, mood, reminder date, and theme preference for each letter. This information is stored locally on your device using SwiftData.

The app may also store lightweight settings such as onboarding status, premium unlock state, and theme selection.

2) How your data is stored

The app is designed around on-device storage only. There is no backend server, no account system, and no cloud profile managed by us for core functionality.

  • Letters are saved locally with SwiftData.
  • Deletion removes the local record from the device.
  • Restarting the app does not erase your letters.

3) Local notifications

If you choose to schedule a future reminder, the app may ask for notification permission. Local notifications are used only to remind you that a letter is ready to re-read.

Reminder details: notification title: A letter is ready. Notification body: Your unsent letter is waiting for you.
  • You can disable notifications in iOS Settings at any time.
  • If you edit or remove a reminder date, the previous notification can be canceled and rescheduled.
  • We do not use notifications for marketing or advertising.

4) Premium placeholder

A future premium experience may unlock extra themes. For V1, premium state is mocked locally with @AppStorage("isPremiumUnlocked") and is not tied to a payment backend.

  • Free theme: Classic.
  • Premium placeholders: Healing, Breakup, Self-love, Moving on.
  • No payment information is stored by this app.

5) Your choices and rights

You can edit or delete letters at any time. You can also change themes, disable reminder permissions, or delete the app to remove local data from your device.

Because the app does not use accounts or a server-side profile for core features, there is no online inbox to manage and no login to recover.

6) Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app evolves. When we do, the effective date at the top of this page will change.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact the app publisher through the app listing or developer support page.
