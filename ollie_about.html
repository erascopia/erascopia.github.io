tiny tunnels, big adventures

Ollie Ant City is a cozy kids game about Ollie the ant and his little world underground. explore tunnels, help friends, and play short, gentle mini games designed for calm focus and fun.

kid-friendly simple puzzles cozy story moments offline-first

what you can do

  • play quick mini games (tunnels, sorting, odd one out, and more)
  • unlock story moments with Ollie and friends
  • collect cute items like berries, seeds, and little treasures
  • practice attention, patterns, and gentle problem solving

what the game does not do

  • does not require an account
  • does not include social features or chat
  • does not collect location data
  • does not show third-party ads

parents - quick notes

Ollie Ant City is built to be calm and safe. the game is designed around short sessions, simple goals, and friendly feedback.

tip: for quieter play, lower sound effects and music in the in-game settings. if you want to fully disable sound, you can also use iOS Settings → Sounds.
