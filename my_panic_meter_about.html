FAQ

Does the app store or upload my voice/text?

Panic Meter is designed for privacy. Processing is intended to run on device. If you choose to share diagnostic logs with support, only the information you explicitly send is shared.

Why does my panic score show 0?

Most commonly, the input is empty or not submitted. Type a short entry and tap Analyze. For voice input, confirm microphone permission is enabled and try a longer phrase.

Is this medical or diagnostic?

No. Panic Meter provides a descriptive reflection of intensity signals in your input. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations.

How do I restore Pro after reinstalling?

Open the app → Paywall → tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you’re signed in with the same Apple ID used to purchase.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Go to Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions and choose Panic Meter. Cancel anytime (access remains until the end of the billing period).

Subscriptions

Pro is optional and provides expanded context, mood labels, ambient listening, and customization. Subscriptions auto-renew unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

Billing & renewal: Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. Renewal is charged within 24 hours prior to the end of the current period.

Restore purchases

If Pro doesn’t unlock after purchase, tap Restore Purchases on the paywall. Ensure you’re logged into the same Apple ID.
